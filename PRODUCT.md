# The Saints Chapel

## Register

brand

## Users

Chapel and exhibition visitors, Catholic families, young people, and curious readers who want to meet the saints through portraiture, short biographies, and prayer. The experience must work equally well on a visitor's phone and on a larger display in a quiet devotional setting.

## Product Purpose

The Saints Chapel presents a focused collection of saints, their lives, their feast days, and trustworthy intercession prayers. It helps visitors move from recognition to reflection: choose a saint, encounter the person through an image and concise story, then pray. Supporting teaching explains Catholic veneration of relics without distracting from that central journey.

## Brand Personality

Reverent, archival, contemplative. The experience should feel like entering a carefully prepared chapel exhibition: warm enough to welcome, restrained enough to support prayer, and confident without spectacle.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard, an entertainment catalogue, or a brightly coloured youth campaign. Avoid disconnected accent panels, decorative glow, glass effects, busy ornament, excessive cards, or visual treatments that compete with the portraits and prayers.

## Design Principles

1. Let each saint's portrait carry the emotional focus.
2. Use the chapel's navy, ivory, and gold language consistently so colour supports contemplation.
3. Make the journey from saint selection to prayer immediate and unambiguous.
4. Prefer editorial restraint and generous reading rhythm over decorative interface chrome.
5. Treat historical images, prayer attribution, and source links with clarity and respect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve semantic heading order, keyboard-visible focus, useful image alternatives, comfortable reading sizes and line lengths, and touch targets suitable for mobile use. Honour reduced-motion preferences and never rely on colour alone to convey meaning.
